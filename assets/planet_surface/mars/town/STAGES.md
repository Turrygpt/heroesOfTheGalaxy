Созданы встроенным ImageGen из master_v1.png. Максимальные уровни используют сам оригинал без перекрывающих слоёв. Ранние стадии выводятся локальными масками с растушёвкой 9 пикселей; генеративная реконструкция внутри масок может отличаться в мелких деталях ландшафта.

## empty_v2.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this exact Mars town panorama as a registered layer for a 2D game. Remove ALL buildings, ships, cranes, weapons, shield tower, central fortress, observatory, market tents, bar, vault. Reconstruct the vacant terrain and empty terraces hidden behind them. Keep central fortress ROCK foundation and platform exactly where it was, do not remove its support cliff. No buildings remain. CRITICAL: preserve exact canvas aspect ratio, camera, perspective, placement, scale, sunlight, color grading. Every cliff, terrace edge, bridge, road, horizon, foreground rail and cable must remain in exactly the same pixels. No composition changes. No new art style. No text. Output full wide panorama, same framing as reference. Buildings must be embedded in this same landscape. This is a localized architectural edit, not a redesign.
